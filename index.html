Cypher Labs is an independent software studio. We build focused, private-first products for people who want their devices to work for them, not the other way around.

01 — About

Who we are

Cypher Labs LLC is a Wyoming-registered studio owned and operated by a single founder. We ship a small number of products with long-term care rather than a large portfolio with none. Our design principles are simple: keep sensitive data on device whenever possible, respect the user's attention, and be honest about what the software can and cannot do.
