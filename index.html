02 — Approach

How we build.

We ship a small number of products with long-term care rather than a large portfolio with none. Three principles, no exceptions.

01

Private by default

Sensitive content stays on your device wherever it can. When it has to leave, we name what and why, in plain language.

02

Focused, not featured

We say no to most things so we can say yes, deeply, to the few that matter. Every feature earns its place.

03

Honest about limits

No dark patterns, no manufactured urgency. We tell you what the software can and can't do, before you install.
